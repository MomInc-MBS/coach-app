MYR5 Coach privacy

Updated September 9, 2026. This page describes the MYR5 Coach web app at myr5.mominc.online, operated by Mom Inc.

Signing in

Clerk handles sign-in, email verification codes and login sessions. If you choose Google, the requested information is your basic profile, email address and account identity. This Coach login does not request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files or YouTube account. Coach uses a verified account identifier to keep your saved records separate from other users.

Existing users can continue with ChatGPT. If you choose to connect an existing Coach to a new login, the app records the connection after you sign in to both accounts.

What Coach saves

Information you submit may include your goal, session length, movement limits, optional setup answers, character appearance, workout records, meals and nutrition entries, and reminder preferences. These records are stored online so they can be restored on another device. If you enable notifications, the app also stores the device's push subscription and your schedule and time zone.

Your camera and photos

Workout camera analysis and food-photo recognition run in your browser. The Coach app does not upload the camera video or selected food photo to its account database. Workout results and the meal details you confirm are saved when you submit them. Recognition software and models are downloaded from external hosting services, which receive ordinary network request information.

Storage and service providers

Coach uses browser storage and cookies for sign-in, settings, offline files and setup transfers. Its online app and data storage use OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. Clerk and its email providers deliver authentication; Google handles Google sign-in. Device push services deliver notifications when enabled. These services receive information needed to provide their functions and may process technical and security logs.

A temporary installation transfer expires after one hour. Your saved Coach account, appearance and workout history are separate from that temporary transfer and remain available until you clear the saved data.

Your choices

In Coach's account settings, use Download my data to export your saved records or Delete Coach data to remove the app's saved Coach records and reminders. Clearing Coach data does not delete your Google, ChatGPT or Clerk login account; the login connection can remain. You can turn off notifications and camera access in your device or browser settings. Clearing browser storage removes local copies.
